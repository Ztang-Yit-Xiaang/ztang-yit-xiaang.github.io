## Observed outcomes, hidden preferences
The MCM 2026 Problem C team project studies a competition in which judge scores and eliminations are observed while fan votes are latent. The repository includes reconstruction notebooks, comparisons of elimination mechanisms, explanatory modeling, and a report.

## Attribution and evidence
This is a team project hosted in my repository. The public attribution does not establish individual responsibility for each modeling component, so this page does not assign those components to me. The notebooks and report are the inspectable project artifacts; they have not been rerun for this animation update.

## Explore the original report figures
The explorer displays the team's original report figures, including recorded model performance and comparisons between elimination rules. Select a figure and inspect its existing details. Values, labels, and uncertainty marks remain part of the saved figures; they were not regenerated for this portfolio.

Fan-vote shares remain inferred quantities rather than observed ground truth. The report's error bars describe seasonal standard deviations, not a newly computed confidence interval. Browsing these figures does not rerun the notebooks or establish a new fairness result.
